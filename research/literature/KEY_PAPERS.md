# Key Papers to Reference

## Foundational Works

### Normative Modeling (Idea 1)
1. Marquand et al. (2019) — "Conceptualizing mental disorders as deviations from normative functioning" — *Molecular Psychiatry*
2. Rutherford et al. (2022) — PCNtoolkit: normative modeling framework — *eLife*
3. Bethlehem et al. (2022) — Brain charts for the human lifespan — *Nature*
4. Wolfers et al. (2018) — Mapping the heterogeneous phenotype of schizophrenia and bipolar disorder using normative models — *JAMA Psychiatry*

### Self-Supervised Learning for fMRI (Idea 2)
5. BrainGFM (2025) — Graph foundation model with prompt tuning — *arXiv:2506.02044*
6. NeuroSTORM (2025) — Foundation model for 50K+ subjects — *arXiv:2506.11167*
7. BrainLM (2024) — Pretrained on 6,700hrs of fMRI — *Nature Machine Intelligence*
8. GCDA (2025) — Graph contrastive domain adaptation for fMRI — *PMC11875923*
9. fMRI-PTE (2024) — Cross-subject pretrained transformer — *arXiv*

### Brain Age (Idea 3)
10. Cole & Franke (2017) — Brain age prediction review — *Trends in Neurosciences*
11. Dafflon et al. (2025) — Simpler models MORE sensitive for brain age clinical deviations — *PLOS Biology*
12. Kaufmann et al. (2019) — Brain age in mental disorders — *Nature Neuroscience*

## Recent Methods (2024-2025)

### GNNs for Brain Connectivity
13. BrainGNN (Li et al., 2021) — ROI-aware graph convolutions — *Medical Image Analysis*
14. Brain Network Transformer (Kan et al., 2022) — Orthonormal clustering readout — *NeurIPS*
15. BPI-GNN (2023) — Prototype-based interpretable GNN — *NeuroImage*
16. DynBrainGNN (2024) — Spatio-temporal attention on dynamic connectomes
17. MHNet (2025) — Multi-view high-order network — *J Digital Imaging*

### The "Simple Models Win" Evidence
18. Thomas et al. (2024) — MLP matches complex deep models for fMRI classification — *NeuroImage*
19. Schulz et al. (2020) — Different scaling of linear models and deep learning — *Nature Communications*

### Clinical Translation Gaps
20. Sanfelici et al. (2023) — 517 neuroimaging-AI studies, 83% high risk of bias — *Nature Mental Health*
21. Botvinik-Nezer et al. (2020) — 70 teams, one dataset → wildly different results — *Nature*
22. Chen et al. (2023) — Meta-analysis of 176 MDD classification studies — publication bias exposed

### Conformal Prediction / Uncertainty
23. Angelopoulos & Bates (2023) — Conformal prediction tutorial — *FTML*
24. Lu et al. (2023) — Conformal prediction for medical imaging

### Cross-Disorder / Transdiagnostic
25. Xia et al. (2018) — Linked dimensions of psychopathology and connectivity — *Nature Communications*
26. Kebets et al. (2019) — Transdiagnostic brain-behavior mapping — *eLife*
